License and Use of the Data

Integrity of the Original Data

Avalanche bulletins are safety-relevant official information. The creation of independent works based on this data is expressly encouraged. These include, in particular, commentaries, additions, corrections, translations, analyses and visualizations.

The following applies to the reproduction, distribution and adaptation of avalanche bulletins, event reports and blog texts:

  • Content that is reproduced as an avalanche bulletin or as a publication of an Avalanche Warning Service must be reproduced unchanged.
  • Changes must be indicated.
  • Original information must be clearly distinguished from additions, corrections, translations and own assessments.
  • Adapted or extended versions must not be presented as original or official publications of the Avalanche Warning Services and must not give the impression of being endorsed by them.
  • The source must be cited and, where possible, a link to the original must be provided.

These obligations arise from the terms of CC BY 4.0. In the event of a violation, the rights granted under the license terminate automatically.

Fair Use Policy

Direct requests from applications to our servers are permitted within the scope of customary use and normal user interaction. Extensive data retrieval and bulk downloads should, where possible, be carried out outside peak usage times so as not to impair the availability and performance of our services. Requests must be identified by a unique User-Agent.

When caching content, please note that bulletins may be updated at any time. To avoid the use of outdated information, we therefore recommend regularly checking whether the data is up to date and, in particular, retrieving the current version before any further use.
